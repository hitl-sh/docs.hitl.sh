# Billing & Plans

HITL uses **usage-based billing** — you're charged when your requests run out, not on a monthly cycle. You only pay for what you use.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Requests | Best For |
|------|-------|----------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | 100 | Getting started — no credit card required |
| **Starter** | $9.99 | 300 | Small projects, auto-renews so you're never locked out |
| **Pro** | $29.99 | 1,000 | Professionals and growing teams |
| **Max** | $99.99 | 5,000 | Large teams with high-volume needs |
| **Enterprise** | $299.99 | 15,000 | Full org management, compliance, and dedicated support |

<Info>
Prices are per request bucket, not per month. You're only charged when your current bucket runs out.
</Info>

## How It Works

### Auto-Charge

When your requests run out, HITL automatically charges your saved payment method for a fresh bucket at your current plan's rate. Your usage counter resets and you can continue immediately.

```
Requests exhausted → Auto-charge → Bucket refilled → Continue working
```

No downtime, no manual action needed.

### Free Plan

Free users get 100 requests to try the platform. Once exhausted, you'll need to upgrade to a paid plan to continue. No credit card is required to start.

## Upgrading

Click **Upgrade** on any plan card in the Billing page. 

- **Free to paid**: You'll be redirected to Stripe Checkout to enter payment details.
- **Paid to higher tier**: Your plan upgrades immediately. Your remaining requests are kept, and your next auto-charge will be at the new rate.

## Downgrading

Downgrades are **deferred** — you keep your current plan and remaining requests until they run out.

<Steps>
<Step title="Choose a lower plan">
Click **Downgrade** on the plan you want to switch to.
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm the downgrade">
Review what will happen: you keep your remaining requests, and your next auto-charge will be at the lower rate.
</Step>

<Step title="Use your remaining requests">
Continue using your current plan until your requests are exhausted.
</Step>

<Step title="Auto-charge at the new rate">
When your requests run out, you're charged at the new (lower) plan's rate and receive that plan's request bucket.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
You can cancel a pending downgrade at any time from the Billing page. You'll stay on your current plan.
</Info>

## Usage Tab

The Usage tab on the Billing page shows:

- **Requests used** — Current count vs. your plan limit
- **Progress bar** — Visual indicator of consumption
- **Plan name** — Your current active plan
- **Auto-renew info** — What happens when requests run out

If you have a pending downgrade, the Usage tab shows an amber banner indicating which plan you're switching to, and the progress bar turns amber.

## Managing Your Billing

Navigate to **Billing** in the sidebar to:

- View your current usage and remaining requests
- Compare and switch between plans
- See pending downgrades and cancel them
- Manage payment methods (for legacy subscribers)
