Every plan has every workflow step type, all triggers and the reviewer apps. Plans differ in how many review requests you can send each month and how many humans you can have.

| | Free | Starter | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $0 | $10 / month | $30 / month | Custom |
| **Review requests / month** | 500 | 2,000 | 5,000 | 50,000 |
| **Humans** | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 |
| **Support** | Community | Email | Priority | Priority, with SLAs |

A **review request** is one question a review step sends: a run that passes two review steps uses two. Humans are the people in your directory, you included.

## Change your plan

Open **Billing** in [my.hitl.sh](https://my.hitl.sh). It shows your plan, the requests and humans used this period, and the plans you can switch to. Starter and Pro are monthly subscriptions paid by card; switching between them takes effect at once. For Enterprise, use **Contact us**.

Prices are in USD. Billing questions: [hello@hitl.sh](mailto:hello@hitl.sh).
