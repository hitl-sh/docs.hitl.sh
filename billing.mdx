# Billing & Plans

HITL.sh offers two plans: a generous free tier to get started, and a custom Enterprise plan for teams that need more.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Responses | Resets | Best For |
|------|-------|-----------|--------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | 500 / month | Monthly | Getting started, small projects, individual use |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Custom | Teams, high volume, dedicated support, compliance |

<Info>
The Free plan resets every month. You get 500 responses per month at no cost, no credit card required.
</Info>

## Free Plan

The Free plan includes:

- **500 responses per month** -- resets on the first of each month
- Full access to the workflow designer
- All node types (trigger, human review, AI, condition, transform, action)
- Unlimited workflows
- Humans management (Universe, Groups, People)
- Mobile app access
- Community support via Discord

No credit card is required. Sign up at [my.hitl.sh](https://my.hitl.sh) and start building workflows immediately.

## Enterprise Plan

For teams that need higher volume, dedicated support, or compliance features, contact us for a custom Enterprise plan.

Enterprise includes everything in Free, plus:

- **Custom response volume** tailored to your needs
- **Organization management** with roles and permissions
- **Priority support** with dedicated account manager
- **Custom audit retention** for compliance requirements
- **SLA guarantees** for uptime and response times
- **SSO / SAML** integration

<Card title="Contact Us" icon="envelope" href="mailto:team@hitl.sh">
Reach out to team@hitl.sh to discuss Enterprise pricing for your team.
</Card>

## Checking Your Usage

Navigate to **Billing** in the dashboard sidebar to see:

- **Responses used** this month vs. your plan limit
- **Progress bar** showing consumption
- **Reset date** for when your monthly count resets
