# Roles & Permissions

HITL.sh uses a 4-tier role model to control access within organizations. Each role has a defined set of capabilities, ensuring team members can only perform actions appropriate to their responsibilities.

## Role Hierarchy

Roles are hierarchical — higher-tier roles inherit all capabilities of lower tiers.

| Role | Level | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **owner** | 4 | Full control over the organization, billing, and settings. One per organization. |
| **admin** | 3 | Manages members, loops, and most settings. Cannot transfer ownership or delete the organization. |
| **member** | 2 | Active reviewer who can respond to requests and participate in loops. |
| **viewer** | 1 | Read-only access to view requests, responses, and analytics. |

## Capabilities by Role

### Owner

<Card title="Owner" icon="crown">
The organization creator with full control. There is exactly one owner per organization.
</Card>

- Everything an admin can do, plus:
- Transfer organization ownership
- Delete the organization
- Manage billing and subscription
- Change the organization plan (Business / Enterprise)

### Admin

<Card title="Admin" icon="user-shield">
Team leads and managers who configure workflows and manage the team.
</Card>

- Everything a member can do, plus:
- Invite and remove members
- Change member roles (except promoting to owner)
- Create, update, and delete loops
- Configure organization settings
- Manage API keys
- Generate and revoke invite codes
- View audit logs

### Member

<Card title="Member" icon="user">
Active reviewers who participate in loops and respond to requests.
</Card>

- Everything a viewer can do, plus:
- Respond to requests assigned to their loops
- Submit approval chain decisions
- Join loops via invite codes
- Access the mobile app with full review capabilities

### Viewer

<Card title="Viewer" icon="eye">
Stakeholders who need visibility into review activity without participating.
</Card>

- View requests and their statuses
- View responses and decisions
- View loop membership and configuration
- Access analytics dashboards (read-only)
- Cannot respond to requests or modify any settings

| Capability | Viewer | Member | Admin | Owner |

| View requests & responses | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| View analytics | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Respond to requests | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Submit approval decisions | — | Yes | Yes | Yes |

| Change member roles | — | — | Yes | Yes |

## Assigning Roles

### During Invitation

When inviting a member, admins and owners can specify the role:

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Organization Settings">
Navigate to your organization's settings page in the dashboard.
</Step>

<Step title="Go to Members">
Select the **Members** tab to view current members and invite new ones.
</Step>

<Step title="Invite with Role">
Enter the invitee's email and select the role (viewer, member, or admin). Click **Send Invite**.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Via Invite Code

Members who join via an invite code are automatically assigned the **member** role. An admin or owner can change their role after they join.

### Changing Roles

Admins and owners can change any member's role from the Members tab:

1. Find the member in the members list
2. Click the role dropdown next to their name
3. Select the new role
4. The change takes effect immediately

<Note>
Admins cannot promote members to **owner** — only the current owner can transfer ownership. Admins also cannot demote other admins; only the owner can do this.
</Note>

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Principle of Least Privilege">
Assign the minimum role needed for each team member's responsibilities. Start with **viewer** or **member** and promote as needed rather than defaulting to admin access.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Limit Admin Count">
Keep the number of admins small — ideally 2-3 per organization. Too many admins increases the risk of accidental configuration changes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Use Viewers for Stakeholders">
Product managers, executives, or clients who need to monitor review activity but shouldn't participate should be assigned the **viewer** role.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Audit Role Changes">
Enterprise organizations should periodically review the members list and ensure roles still match current responsibilities. Audit logs track all role changes for compliance.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
